1. **Over-reliance on benchmark metrics**: The development of language models is often driven by benchmark metrics, such as perplexity and accuracy. While these metrics provide a useful measure of technical proficiency, they can overlook the importance of emotional resonance and authenticity.
2. **Limited contextual understanding**: Current language models often struggle to capture the nuances of human context, leading to responses that are detached or unengaging.
3. **Lack of diversity and complexity**: The training data used to develop language models is often limited to a narrow range of sources and styles, resulting in models that lack diversity and complexity.
