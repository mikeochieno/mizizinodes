## Introduction to the Subaru Sambar 360
The Subaru Sambar 360, produced from 1961 to 1973, is a kei car that was designed to navigate Japan's narrow streets and provide an affordable, practical mode of transportation. The 1969 model, in particular, boasts a 356cc, two-stroke, two-cylinder engine, producing 18 horsepower and 22 lb-ft of torque. Weighing in at a mere 1,100 pounds, this van can reach a top speed of 65 mph, with a 0-60 time of around 30 seconds. While these specs may seem modest by today's standards, the Sambar 360's lightweight construction and compact size make it an agile and charming vehicle to drive.
