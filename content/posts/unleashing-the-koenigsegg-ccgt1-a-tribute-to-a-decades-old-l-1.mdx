## Introduction to the Koenigsegg CCGT1
The Koenigsegg CCGT1 is a limited-edition hypercar that celebrates the brand's rich racing heritage and its decades-old dream of competing in the 24 Hours of Le Mans. With only 10 units produced, this car is a rare gem that combines exceptional performance, unique design, and a deep connection to the world of motorsports. Powered by a 5.0-liter twin-turbo V8 engine, the CCGT1 produces an impressive 900 horsepower and 774 lb-ft of torque, allowing it to accelerate from 0-60 mph in just 2.8 seconds and reach a top speed of 240 mph.
