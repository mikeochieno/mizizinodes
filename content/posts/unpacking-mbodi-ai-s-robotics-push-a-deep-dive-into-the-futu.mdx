## Critical Analysis: Limitations and Open Questions
While the development of AI agents is an exciting and rapidly evolving field, there are several limitations and open questions that need to be addressed. One of the primary challenges is the issue of **explainability**, which refers to the ability to understand and interpret the decision-making processes of AI agents. As these agents become more complex and autonomous, it will be essential to develop methods for explaining their behavior and ensuring that they are aligned with human values.

Other key challenges include:

* **Safety and security**: The development of AI agents raises significant safety and security concerns, particularly in areas like robotics and healthcare.
* **Scalability**: As AI agents become more sophisticated, they will require more powerful computing hardware and larger amounts of training data.
* **Ethics**: The use of AI agents raises important ethical questions about accountability, transparency, and fairness.

The development of AI agents will have a significant impact on a range of industries and applications, from robotics and healthcare to education and entertainment. Some potential use cases include:
