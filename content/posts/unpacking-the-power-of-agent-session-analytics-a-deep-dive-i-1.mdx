## Introduction to MCP Evals
The concept of Model Central Platforms (MCP) has been gaining traction in recent years, particularly with the rise of large language models (LLMs) like GPT, Claude, and Gemini. MCPs aim to provide a unified framework for developing, deploying, and managing AI models, allowing for greater efficiency, scalability, and collaboration. The introduction of product analytics and evaluations for agent sessions on MCP marks a significant step forward in this direction. By enabling developers to assess and optimize agent performance in a standardized way, MCP evals have the potential to accelerate the development of more sophisticated AI systems.

While MCP evals offer significant advantages over traditional evaluation methods, they are not without limitations and trade-offs. Additionally, the standardized framework of MCP evals may not be suitable for all types of AI models or applications, particularly those requiring highly customized or domain-specific evaluation metrics.

To address these concerns, developers and researchers must be aware of the potential limitations and trade-offs of MCP evals and use them in conjunction with other evaluation methods and metrics.
